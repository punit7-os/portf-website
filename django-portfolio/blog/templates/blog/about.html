This Blog Isn’t About Tech.
It’s About What Tech Does for Business.

If you’re a founder, business owner, or decision-maker trying to figure out what to build, why to build it, and when — you’re in the right place.

Why This Blog Exists

Most content online falls into one of two extremes.

  • • Highly technical blogs that lose business readers in the first paragraph
  • • Vague “digital transformation” articles that say everything — and nothing

This blog exists to sit right in the middle. To explain how technology decisions impact revenue, time, scalability, and control — without forcing you to learn code.

Who This Blog Is For

You’ll get the most value here if you are:

  • A business owner tired of manual processes and spreadsheets
  • A founder planning a product but unsure what features actually matter
  • An agency or consultant exploring automation for clients
  • A decision-maker who wants clarity before hiring a developer

Who I Am

I’m Punit Patel, a developer who doesn’t start with tools — I start with problems.

I build systems using Django, automation, and modern frontend tools, but more importantly, I focus on why a feature exists and how it helps the business long-term.

This blog complements my portfolio by showing not just what I build, but how I think when designing real-world solutions.
